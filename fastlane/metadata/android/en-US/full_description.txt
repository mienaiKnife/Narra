Narra turns text into audio and plays it like a podcast. Subscribe to RSS
feeds, import EPUB books, or save web articles, then queue them up and
listen without ads, accounts, or tracking.

FEATURES

- RSS and Atom feeds: subscribe to blogs and fetch new articles
  automatically.
- EPUB import: listen to DRM-free ebooks.
- Web article import: extract clean, readable text from a URL.
- Listening queue: play, reorder, and autoplay items in sequence.
- Two TTS engines: Android's built-in voices, or optional on-device AI
  voices powered by Sherpa-ONNX.
- Background playback with notification and lock-screen controls.
- Home screen widget with playback controls and queue status.
- Sleep timer, adjustable playback speed, and customizable skip
  durations.
- Word highlighting while reading along.
- OPML import and export for your feed list.
- JSON backup and restore, plus optional auto-export for syncing between
  devices with a file-sync tool such as Syncthing.

PRIVACY

Narra requires no account and has no analytics, ads, or crash reporting.
Your feeds, articles, and listening history stay on your device, stored in
an encrypted database.

The optional AI voices run entirely on the device. Their voice models are
large, so Narra downloads them only when you choose one in Settings >
Voices, and the download can be restricted to Wi-Fi. Android's built-in
TTS works without downloading anything in Narra.
