Privacy fix: feed and article addresses are no longer sent to a third-party
favicon service. Site icons now come from the site's own /favicon.ico, with
the letter avatar as fallback, and article downloads send the page's own
origin as the Referer instead of google.com.

Also in this update: feed refresh reports which feeds failed, typographic
dashes split words correctly in the reader, and bundled asset license texts
can be read in full.
